Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about coaching.

In a free initial consultation, we get to know each other and clarify your goals. After that, we schedule regular sessions (60–90 minutes) where we work on your topics using proven methods. Between sessions, you receive exercises and impulses for deeper reflection.

The duration depends on your goals and topics. Typically, a coaching process comprises 6–12 sessions over a period of 3–6 months. For specific individual topics, fewer sessions may be sufficient.

Yes! Online coaching via video call is just as effective as in-person coaching. It offers you maximum flexibility — you can participate from anywhere, even while traveling for business or working from home.

The costs depend on the type and scope of coaching. In the free initial consultation, I will provide you with an individual quote. For corporate clients, I also offer package rates and framework agreements.

Coaching is suitable for anyone who wants to develop further — whether executives, entrepreneurs, employees or people going through personal transitions. The prerequisite is a willingness to engage in the process and actively work toward your own goals.
