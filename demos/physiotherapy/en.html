Our Services

Holistic therapy — tailored to your needs.

🧢

Physiotherapy

Targeted exercises and techniques to restore and improve your mobility — after injury, surgery or for chronic conditions.

🖐

Manual Therapy

Specialised hands-on techniques for treating dysfunctions in joints, muscles and nerves — gentle yet effective.

🏃

Sports Physiotherapy

Prevention, rehabilitation and performance optimisation for athletes of all levels — from weekend joggers to competitive athletes.

💧

Lymphatic Drainage

Gentle, rhythmic massage technique to promote lymphatic flow — for swelling, after surgery or for lymphoedema.

🧬

Back Therapy

Specialised treatment for back pain, disc problems and postural issues — for a strong, pain-free back.

🏠

Home Visits

Physiotherapy at your home — for patients with limited mobility. With a doctor's prescription and by appointment.
