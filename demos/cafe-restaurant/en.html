Family Business with Heart

Café Goldstück is a family-run café focused on quality, sustainability and hospitality. Our ingredients come from the region — fresh, seasonal, honest.

We believe that great coffee and a warm smile make all the difference. Whether alone with a book, working, or with friends — you are welcome here.
