Our Story

A Place Built on Heart and Craft

In 2012, the Meier family opened Café Goldstück with a simple idea: honest food, thoughtful details, and an atmosphere where everyone feels at home.

Today we bake fresh every day, source ingredients from regional producers, and roast our coffee in collaboration with a local artisan roastery.

"Great food starts where great ingredients and passion come together."
